Application matrix

Match each asset class to its challenge, value, and assessment inputs.

COMMERCIAL

Commercial buildings

Building challenge
High utility tariffs and strict urban fire codes.
ThermoVerse value
Immediate energy audits plus future active thermal load-shifting via LATCHES.
Assessment inputs needed
Operating hours, utility tariff data, and HVAC loads.
INDUSTRIAL

Industrial sites

Building challenge
Thermal bridging causing Energy Use Intensity (EUI) spikes and equipment wear.
ThermoVerse value
MEP assessments to optimize process heat plus localized thermal buffering via LATCHES.
Assessment inputs needed
Thermal loads, equipment specifications, and site conditions.
MULTIFAMILY

Multifamily properties

Building challenge
Tenant thermal discomfort and high common-area HVAC costs.
ThermoVerse value
Comprehensive BPI energy assessments plus zero-footprint ceiling thermal regulation.
Assessment inputs needed
Floor plans, HVAC layout, and occupancy data.

Submitting an inquiry does not create a POC agreement. POC scope, schedule, equipment, data access, costs, confidentiality, measurement methods, publicity rights, and each party’s responsibilities must be agreed separately in writing.
