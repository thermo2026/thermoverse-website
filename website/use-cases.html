HOW IT WORKS

A clear, accountable 4-step path from data to execution.

Every engagement follows an accountable framework designed to minimize disruption to building tenants while identifying high-impact energy savings.

STEP 01 / INTAKE

Utility & Data Intake

We review historical electricity and gas utility records alongside facility mechanical schedules to model preliminary consumption baselines.

STEP 02 / DIAGNOSTICS

On-Site BPI Assessment

Certified BPI specialists conduct on-site physical inspections, building envelope leakage diagnostics, and thermal imaging checks.

STEP 03 / ROADMAP

ECM & Rebate Roadmap

We deliver prioritized Energy Conservation Measures with quantified ROI payback periods, cross-referenced with eligible utility and state rebates.

STEP 04 / DELIVERY

Vetted Contractor Delivery

We connect building owners with vetted local clean energy trade contractors to execute installations cleanly and verify performance.
