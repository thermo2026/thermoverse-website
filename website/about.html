02 · Goal and Mission

Our Mission

01

As electrical demands surge from data centers and electric mobility, optimizing building energy use is critical for ensuring sustainable smart cities. We redirect peak HVAC demand to unlock municipal grid capacity.

Our Vision

02

We develop the technologies that harness waste heat to significantly reduce HVAC operational load, ensuring commercial and multifamily properties are highly efficient, resilient, and grid-ready.

Our Impact

03

Vital for addressing rising electrical loads from electric vehicles and AI data centers, ensuring a resilient, interoperable energy network — from the building ceiling to the municipal utility grid.

Active SIMs

04

A patented family of compact, self-contained units that embed sensing, thermal energy storage, and control directly in ceilings and walls, unlocking active load flexibility from ceiling to grid.
