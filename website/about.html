01 · MISSION & POSITIONING

Our goals

01 / 04

Preserve 100% of rentable floor space.

By embedding LATCHES™ modular phase-change units seamlessly into existing ceiling plenums and architectural cavities, dense commercial properties deploy massive thermal storage with zero rentable square footage consumed.

0 SF Rentable Area Consumed

Inherently non-flammable solid-state safety.

Unlike chemical battery storage systems (BESS) subject to thermal runaway risks, toxic venting, and stringent municipal fire codes, our solid-state phase-change composite is chemically inert and completely safe for occupied high-rises.

Non-Toxic · Zero Thermal Runaway Risk

Dynamic grid resilience & peak shifting.

Smart predictive edge algorithms orchestrate automated thermal charging during off-peak windows and discharge during peak demand hours—curbing peak utility tariffs while buffering local distribution transformers.

Automated Peak HVAC Load Shifting

Practical action today, deep innovation tomorrow.

Our dual-track approach combines immediate utility cost reductions through ASHRAE Level I–III audits and MEP optimizations today, while concurrently developing proprietary thermal storage assets for smart cities.

From Immediate Audits to Active Storage
