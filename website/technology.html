LATCHES™

The world's most compact, zero-footprint thermal energy storage solution

Solid-state thermal battery designed for dense urban buildings

Urban-safe energy storage

Thermal storage without surrendering floor area.

By integrating thermal storage into existing ceiling or wall areas, it offers an alternative where traditional chemical Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS) may be restricted by urban fire codes or space constraints.

Sensing, storage, and control

Command the timed release of stored thermal energy.

01 / MONITOR

Sense

A State-of-Charge Monitor observes the thermal storage state and relevant building conditions.

02 / EDGE CONTROL

Predict

A predictive edge controller interprets demand, HVAC, and operating signals close to the equipment.

03 / THERMAL CYCLE

Charge or discharge

The controller commands storage cycles and the timed release of thermal energy in the PCMs.

Safe, Clean, Compact

Solid-state thermal storage designed for occupied spaces.

Unlike chemical battery systems (BESS) restricted by dense urban fire codes, LATCHES™ utilizes solid-state Phase Change Materials that are non-toxic and safe for occupied indoor spaces.

THERMAL CAPACITY

102 BTU/SF storage capacity*

LATCHES™ delivers 102 BTU/SF of latent thermal storage, designed to maintain indoor temperature setpoints four hours longer than standard insulation.

*Based on laboratory test results.

Installed up to 8× faster*

Zero-footprint integration with Ceilings.

LATCHES™ is designed for drop-ceiling and wall integration, preserving rentable floor area while placing thermal storage close to the conditioned space.

*Compared to conventional battery energy storage systems (BESS).

Heat-flow control and peak management

BACnet-native control through the building’s existing BAS.

LATCHES™ is designed to integrate with a Building Automation System (BAS) through BACnet-native controls. The building can coordinate thermal charging and discharge with HVAC schedules, peak-demand periods, heat-flow needs, and demand-response strategies.

Heat-flow control

Coordinate stored thermal energy with zone and building conditions.

Peak management

Shift HVAC thermal loads away from peak-demand periods.

Demand response

Use existing BAS signals to support site and program demand-response strategies.

FAQ

Suitability, integration needs, and assessment data.

Clear criteria to evaluate your building’s electrical, structural, and network readiness for LATCHES™ zero-footprint thermal storage.

What network compatibility is required?

A site review confirms the existing BAS, BACnet network, controls architecture, and available connection points.

Can the ceiling support the system?

Ceiling type, available area, structural loading, services, access, and installation conditions must be assessed for each building.

What building data is needed?

Energy Services audits gather operating hours, utility tariffs, HVAC loads, equipment information, floor plans, and occupancy data relevant to qualification.

Which sites are suitable?

Commercial, industrial, and multifamily properties can be evaluated against their energy profile, HVAC configuration, installation conditions, and project goals.
