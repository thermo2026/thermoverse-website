About ThermoVerse Inc.

Bridging Today’s Energy Service Needs and Tomorrow’s Thermal Storage.

ThermoVerse is an urban energy storage startup headquartered in Detroit, Michigan. The company combines immediate building Energy Services with the development of LATCHES for dense urban environments.

Connect with Our Team
01 · Company positioning and mission

Essential grid capacity for dense cities.

ThermoVerse addresses two connected needs: improving the performance of buildings operating today and developing compact thermal storage for cities where floor area and fire-code constraints limit traditional Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS).

02 · Company background

Energy service expertise meets an engineering breakthrough.

The company’s work begins with energy assessments, audits, MEP consulting, and retrofits that help facilities act on immediate performance needs. Its innovation program develops LATCHES—Large-Area Transactive Cooling & Heat Energy Storage—and predictive edge controls for the timed release of stored thermal energy.
