About ThermoVerse Inc.

Bridging Today’s Energy Needs

ThermoVerse is an urban energy storage startup headquartered in Detroit, Michigan. We combine immediate building Energy Services with the breakthrough development of LATCHES™ for dense urban environments.

01 · MISSION & POSITIONING

Essential grid capacity for dense cities.

ThermoVerse addresses two connected needs: improving the operational performance of buildings today and developing compact thermal storage for cities where floor area and fire-code constraints limit traditional chemical battery systems (BESS).

02 · COMPANY BACKGROUND

Energy service expertise meets active thermal innovation.

Our work begins with comprehensive energy assessments, ASHRAE audits, MEP consulting, and retrofits that help facilities act on immediate performance needs. In parallel, our innovation program develops LATCHES™—Large-Area Transactive Cooling & Heat Energy Storage—and predictive edge controls for the timed release of stored thermal energy.
