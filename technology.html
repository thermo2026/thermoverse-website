Suitability, ceiling coverage, and building readiness.

Clear criteria to evaluate your building’s electrical, ceiling, and network readiness for LATCHES™ zero-footprint thermal batteries.

Discuss Your Requirements →

How much ceiling coverage is required to unlock capacity?

It depends on facility load profiles. For example, in a 100,000 sq. ft. commercial facility, approximately 35% ceiling coverage unlocks up to 1 Megawatt-hour (MWh) of active thermal battery headroom—equivalent to a grid-scale battery pack—without surrendering any rentable floor area. LATCHES™ is engineered to scale modularly from a single space to your entire portfolio.

Can standard drop-ceilings support the system?

Yes. Standard commercial 2x2 ft drop-ceiling T-bar grids support up to 13.5 lbs (approx. 3.25 lbs/sq. ft.). LATCHES™ lightweight cassettes weigh comfortably below this threshold (engineered around 10–11 lbs) and are designed to distribute load evenly across standard commercial grids in full compliance with building codes.

What criteria determine building readiness for LATCHES™?

While LATCHES™ can be adapted across diverse architectures, our current deployment focuses on facilities with pre-existing drop-ceiling grid infrastructure with plenum accessibility, and pre-existing BAS connectivity for rapid, seamless commissioning.

What network compatibility is required?

LATCHES™ operates natively over BACnet MS/TP and BACnet/IP protocols, communicating directly with existing Building Automation Systems without requiring proprietary gateways or vendor lock-in.
