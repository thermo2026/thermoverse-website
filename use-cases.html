Welcome to practical building energy management.

A certified BPI energy assessment helps property owners and facility teams make better and faster capital decisions based on verified data. It’s an accountable framework that allows your building to go from identifying utility waste to verified retrofit completion without operational disruption.

It’s not guesswork, it’s building science.

STEP 01 · BASELINE MODELING

Utility & Data Intake

Historical electricity and gas consumption analysis cross-referenced with mechanical operating schedules and utility rate structures.

STEP 02 · CERTIFIED INSPECTION

On-Site BPI Assessment

Certified BPI auditors conduct envelope air-leakage tests, blower door testing, physical walk-throughs, and infrared thermal imaging.

STEP 03 · QUANTIFIED PAYBACK

ECM & Rebate Roadmap

Prioritized Energy Conservation Measures with modeled ROI, matched with eligible utility, federal, and state rebate incentives.

STEP 04 · TURNKEY EXECUTION

Vetted Trade Delivery

Direct matching with certified local clean energy contractors to execute installations cleanly and verify results.
