# ThermoVerse FUTEX 2026 SOW Project Delivery Recommendations

The following revisions clarify responsibilities, reduce execution gaps, and support the timely delivery of the website, CRM, event representation, and academic outreach.

## I. Revisions to Existing Terms

### 1. Project Overview

> The Services include a one-time bilingual optimization of the Company’s website, setup of a basic CRM lead-management workflow, documented academic outreach, FUTEX representation, and demo logistics within the scope stated in this SOW. Academic outreach will be evaluated based on the documented actions and deliverables completed by the Contractor, while third-party responses, partnerships, grant eligibility, grant approval, and funding remain external outcomes.

The Company is responsible for the accuracy and substantiation of all technical, regulatory, product-performance, grant-related, and marketing claims supplied or approved by the Company.

### 3.A Logistics & Equipment Handling

Add:

> Before pickup, the Company shall provide an equipment inventory, photographs, declared replacement value, dimensions, weight, handling instructions, operating instructions, and disclosure of any special transportation requirements. The parties shall document the condition of the demo container at pickup and return to ensure a clear and efficient handover. The Contractor will be responsible for loss or damage caused by the Contractor’s willful misconduct or gross negligence. Inherent defects, normal wear and tear, inadequate packaging, and undisclosed hazards will remain the Company’s responsibility.

### 3.B Expo Representation

Add:

Work requested outside these hours, separate setup or teardown work, or additional event days requires prior written approval and a mutually agreed additional fee. ## II. Additional Terms

### 4. Review and Acceptance

> All review deadlines are based on Taiwan Time (UTC+8). The Contractor will provide review versions on September 12 and September 14, 2026, and the final version on September 16, 2026. The fee includes a maximum of three consolidated revision rounds. Each round begins when the Company submits one consolidated written feedback list and ends when the Contractor delivers the corresponding revised version. Delayed Company feedback will extend the following deadline by the corresponding delay. Material defects that cause the deliverable not to conform to the agreed scope will be corrected without counting as a revision round. New content, changed preferences, out-of-scope requests, and revisions beyond the three included rounds require a written change order.

### 5. Change Control

> Any change to the scope, deliverables, assumptions, schedule, or number of revisions must be agreed in writing by both parties. The Contractor is not required to begin out-of-scope work until the parties approve the corresponding fee and schedule adjustment.

### 6. Company Dependencies and Delay

> The Company shall provide complete materials, access credentials, approved technical evidence, and consolidated feedback according to the agreed schedule. To preserve delivery quality and timing, Company or third-party delays will result in a corresponding schedule adjustment unless the parties agree in writing to reprioritize the scope.

### 7. Intellectual Property

> Upon full payment, the Company will own the final website copy, page layouts, and project-specific CRM configuration created exclusively under this SOW. The Contractor retains ownership of pre-existing tools, templates, methods, know-how, and reusable components, while granting the Company a perpetual license to use components embedded in the final deliverables. The Company warrants that it has the right to use all materials it provides.

### 8. Confidentiality and Personal Data

> Each party will protect non-public business, technical, credential, and personal information received from the other party and use it only to perform this SOW. The Company is responsible for approving the privacy notice, lawful collection purpose, retention period, access policy, and marketing-consent language. Upon completion, the Contractor will return or delete Company credentials and personal-data exports, except where retention is required by law.

### 9. Cancellation, Termination, and Force Majeure

> Either party may terminate this SOW for material breach if the breach is not cured within five business days after written notice, where cure is reasonably possible. If FUTEX is cancelled, postponed, inaccessible, or materially disrupted for reasons outside the Contractor’s control, the Company will pay for completed work, non-cancellable commitments, and approved expenses incurred through the cancellation date.

### 10. Limitation of Responsibility

> To ensure that each party is responsible for matters within its control, third-party platform outages, venue decisions, recipient responses, grant outcomes, product defects, and Company-supplied claims and materials will remain outside the Contractor’s responsibility. Except for willful misconduct, gross negligence, breach of confidentiality, or misuse of personal data, each party’s aggregate liability arising from this SOW will not exceed the total fees paid or payable under this SOW.

### 11. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

> This SOW and any agreement incorporating it shall be governed by the laws of Taiwan (Republic of China), without regard to conflict-of-law principles. The parties agree that the Taiwan Taipei District Court shall have exclusive jurisdiction as the court of first instance over any dispute arising out of or relating to this SOW.
